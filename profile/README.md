Duatic merges innovative hardware and intelligent software to build next-gen robotics, helping
industries overcome labor shortages.

### What we build

- **[DXTR](https://www.duatic.com/dxtr)**: dual-arm mobile manipulator. Picks, moves and places on
  your existing floor layout.
- **[DynaArm](https://www.duatic.com/dynaarm)**: ultra-lightweight arm with quasi-direct drive,
  built for mobile applications.
- **[DuaTorso](https://www.duatic.com/duatorso)**: dual-arm upper body with vision, ready to mount
  on your own platform.
- **[DuaRover](https://www.duatic.com/duarover)**: autonomous mobile base that navigates existing
  warehouse layouts.

Two coordinated arms lift up to 30 kg together; the mobile base carries up to 200 kg on top.

### Open source

Our public repositories cover the ROS 2 tooling, CI/CD infrastructure, and drivers we build
robots on. Most of what runs our robots' upper-level software is ROS 2 based; see individual
repositories for specifics.
